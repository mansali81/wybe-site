Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

WYBE takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and what rights you have — including the specific protections that apply if you are a UAE resident (under the PDPL) or an EU/UK visitor (under GDPR-equivalent standards).

1. Who We Are

WYBE is a personal training, human-performance nutrition, and mind-coaching business operated by Mansoor Ahamad Ali Mohamed, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, WYBE is the data controller of the personal information you submit through this website or during a WYBE service engagement. You can reach us at mansoor@wybe.fit for any privacy-related question or request.

2. What Information We Collect

Contact information

Name, email address, phone number, city/country, and any message content you submit through our contact form, WhatsApp, or Instagram DM.

Health, fitness & body-composition data

When you use the on-site calculators or submit an intake form for a WYBE service, we may collect: gender, age, height, weight, neck/waist/hip measurements, current activity level, training history, injuries or medical conditions you choose to disclose, nutrition goals, and progress photographs you upload.

Health-adjacent data is a special category under both UAE PDPL and GDPR. We only collect what we need to design a safe, effective program for you, and we treat it accordingly.

Payment information

Payment card details are collected and processed by our third-party payment provider (see §4). WYBE receives a transaction reference and the amount paid — not your full card number.

Technical & usage data

Standard log data automatically recorded by our hosting provider (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referrer, timestamp), and — where you have not opted out — aggregate analytics data (see §9 on cookies).

3. How We Use It

We use your personal data only for the purposes below, and only to the extent necessary:

  • To deliver the WYBE service you have booked (program design, session planning, nutrition guidance, progress tracking);
  • To communicate with you about scheduling, changes, invoices, and post-session follow-ups;
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the website or messaging channels;
  • To generate PDF reports and calculator outputs at your explicit request;
  • To improve the safety and quality of the service (e.g. anonymised progress benchmarking);
  • To meet legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations under UAE law.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your health or fitness data for advertising or profiling.

4. Third-Party Tools

To deliver the website and its services, WYBE relies on a limited number of vetted third-party providers. Each is a data processor in their own right and is bound by their own privacy policy:

  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting and content delivery. Processes IP and request-log data.
  • Google LLC — Google Fonts (font delivery) and, if enabled on the page, Google Analytics 4 for aggregate usage measurement.
  • Web3Forms — inbound contact-form delivery, forwarding your submission to the WYBE email inbox.
  • Payment processors — Stripe or an equivalent provider handles card processing when checkout is offered. Their privacy policies apply to the payment step.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram) and Google (YouTube, TikTok) — used only if you choose to contact WYBE through these platforms; the platforms' own policies govern those interactions.

WYBE keeps this list under review and will update it here when a processor is added or removed.

5. Data Storage & Security

Personal data is stored on encrypted-at-rest infrastructure provided by our hosting and processor partners. Access on the WYBE side is limited to Mansoor Ahamad Ali Mohamed and (where operationally needed) a small, named assistant, both bound by confidentiality obligations.

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected — typically:

  • Client intake and program records: for the duration of the engagement + 3 years for follow-up and defence of any claims;
  • Payment records: 7 years, as required for UAE accounting/VAT compliance;
  • Marketing consents (photo, testimonial): until you withdraw consent or the material is retired;
  • Aggregate analytics: rolling 14 months in Google Analytics.

No online system is perfectly secure. In the event of a personal-data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you and (where required) the competent authority in line with applicable law.

6. Your Rights

Regardless of where you live, you can ask WYBE to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Delete data we no longer need to keep;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing (for example, marketing use of a testimonial);
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave, at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Port your data to another provider, in a common machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email mansoor@wybe.fit. We will verify your identity and respond within 30 days. Complex requests may take longer; we will keep you informed.

7. UAE PDPL Compliance (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021)

If you reside in the United Arab Emirates, your personal data is processed under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law ("PDPL"). In addition to the rights set out in §6, PDPL specifically gives you:

  • The right to be informed of the categories of your data being processed, the purposes, and any onward disclosure;
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the UAE Data Office if you consider that our processing infringes the PDPL;
  • The right, where automated decision-making produces a significant effect on you, to request human review;
  • Additional safeguards where your data is transferred to a country not deemed by the UAE Data Office to provide an adequate level of protection.

WYBE relies on the following lawful bases under the PDPL: performance of a contract with you (for service delivery), your explicit consent (for health data and marketing use), and legitimate interests (for website operation and dispute defence) — balanced against your rights and freedoms.

Where personal data is transferred outside the UAE (for example, to Vercel data centres or Google servers), we rely on the transfer safeguards prescribed by the UAE Data Office and, where required, obtain your explicit consent.

8. GDPR Rights (EU/EEA/UK Visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also enjoy the rights set out under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and its UK equivalent. These are functionally aligned with the rights in §6 above, and additionally include:

  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority (for example, the Data Protection Commission in Ireland, the CNIL in France, or the ICO in the United Kingdom);
  • The right to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, and to profiling;
  • Specific protections for cross-border transfers of your data outside the EEA/UK — WYBE relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (or equivalent transfer mechanisms) with its non-EEA processors.

WYBE does not have an EU/UK establishment and, given the volume of EU/UK visitors, is not required to appoint an EU representative. If EU/UK activity grows to the threshold at which appointment is required under GDPR Article 27, this policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Cookies & Similar Technologies

WYBE uses a minimal set of cookies and browser storage:

  • Strictly necessary — session and preference cookies used to make the website work (e.g. remembering that a mobile menu is open). No consent required.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4, if enabled on a given page, sets a first-party cookie to distinguish unique visitors in an aggregate way. You can opt out via your browser settings, a browser extension, or by declining consent where a banner is present.

WYBE does not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.

10. Children

WYBE services are directed at adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a minor has submitted information through the website, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page indicates the most recent revision. Where changes are material, we will notify active clients by email and, where practicable, display a banner on this page for at least 30 days.

12. Contact

For any privacy question, request, or complaint, email mansoor@wybe.fit. Include enough detail to identify the request and — where relevant — proof of identity.
